1. Introduction

Welcome to BuildKnack, a mobile application developed and operated by Tecknack Limited ("Tecknack", "we", "our" or "us").

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect your personal information when you use the BuildKnack mobile application, related services and any associated support services (collectively, the "Service").

BuildKnack is designed to help Builders create project estimates, quotations and contract documents using artificial intelligence together with information provided by the user. Much of the information you create within the Service is stored locally on your device, while certain information is processed or stored securely using trusted cloud service providers to deliver essential functionality.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, how long we retain it, the third parties that help us provide the Service and the rights available to you regarding your personal information.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the Service.

This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the BuildKnack mobile application and any related services operated by Tecknack Limited.

4. Information You Provide

You choose what information you provide when using BuildKnack. This information is used to provide the features you request and to support the operation of the Service.

4.1 Account Information

When you sign in using Apple Sign In or Google Sign-In, we receive information necessary to authenticate your account.

We do not require you to create or manage a separate username or password for BuildKnack.

4.2 Builder Profile

You may provide information including:

  • your name;
  • company name;
  • business address;
  • contact details;
  • trade information; and
  • builder pricing and labour rates.

4.3 Project Information

You may create Jobs within the Service that include:

  • project descriptions;
  • project photographs;
  • measurements;
  • notes;
  • pricing information; and
  • other project-related information.

4.4 Customer Information

You may enter information relating to your customers, such as names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses and other information necessary for preparing estimates, Quotes and Contracts.

4.5 Generated Documents

You may create and edit estimates, Quotes and Contract documents using the Service.

These documents remain under your control and responsibility.

5. Information We Collect Automatically

Some information is collected automatically when you use BuildKnack in order to provide the Service, manage subscriptions, improve performance and protect against misuse.

5.1 Authentication Information

When you sign in using Apple or Google, we process the authentication information necessary to recognise your account and provide access to the Service.

5.2 Subscription Information

We process information relating to your selected plan, subscription status, purchases and remaining usage allowances.

5.3 Usage Information

We collect information relating to how the Service is used, including feature usage, job allowances, AI analysis usage and general application activity.

This information helps us improve the reliability and functionality of the Service.

5.4 Device Information

We may collect limited technical information about your device, such as operating system version, application version and other technical diagnostics necessary for security, troubleshooting and performance monitoring.

5.5 Abuse Prevention

To help prevent misuse of promotional offers and free plan allowances, we generate a one-way cryptographic hash based on your authentication provider identifier.

The original provider identifier is not stored for this purpose. The hashed identifier is used solely for fraud prevention and abuse detection.

6. Information Processed by AI

BuildKnack uses artificial intelligence to assist in generating project estimates based on information provided by the Builder.

6.1 Information Sent for AI Processing

When you request an AI Estimate Analysis, we may securely process:

  • project photographs;
  • project descriptions;
  • Builder pricing and labour rates; and
  • other information necessary to generate the requested estimate.

6.2 Purpose of AI Processing

The information is processed solely for the purpose of generating the estimate requested by you and improving the operation of the AI-assisted features of the Service where permitted by applicable law and our agreements with service providers.

6.3 Human Review

AI-generated estimates are produced automatically.

We do not routinely review the content of your Jobs, photographs, estimates or generated documents unless reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, technical support requested by you or where otherwise required by law.

6.4 AI Accuracy

Artificial intelligence may produce inaccurate or incomplete results. You remain responsible for reviewing and verifying all AI-generated estimates before relying upon them for commercial purposes.

Certain information is stored securely using trusted cloud service providers in order to provide account functionality, subscription management and fraud prevention.

10.1 Cloud Information

  • Builder company profile;

13. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements and protect the security and integrity of BuildKnack.

13.1 Information Stored on Your Device

Information stored locally on your device, including Jobs, photographs, customer information, Quotes, Contracts, estimates, Builder profiles and Builder rates, remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the application or remove it yourself.

13.2 Cloud-Stored Information

Information stored in our cloud services, including subscription information, Builder company profile, Builder rates, usage counters and account information, is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Service or meet legal and operational requirements.

13.3 Fraud Prevention Records

Where you delete your BuildKnack account, the one-way hashed identifier used solely for fraud prevention and abuse detection may be retained for up to six (6) months after account deletion.

This allows us to prevent repeated abuse of free plan allowances while avoiding retention of your original authentication provider identifier.

13.4 Legal Requirements

We may retain certain information for longer where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, investigate fraud or enforce our legal rights.

14. Account Deletion

You may delete your BuildKnack account at any time using the account management features available within the application.

14.1 What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you request account deletion, we will permanently delete or anonymise your cloud-based personal information within a reasonable period unless we are required to retain certain information by law.

14.2 Fraud Prevention Record

The one-way hashed identifier used solely for abuse prevention may be retained for up to six (6) months after account deletion before being permanently removed.

14.3 Local Device Information

Deleting your BuildKnack account does not automatically remove information stored locally on your device.

You may remove locally stored information by deleting it within the application or uninstalling the application from your device.
